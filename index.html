Good to know

FAQ

How do I book a ride?

Use the Book a Ride form on this page. Add your pickup, destination, timing, and any trip details we should know.

Can I bring chairs, coolers, and umbrellas?

Tell us what beach gear you’re bringing when you request your ride. We’ll confirm the vehicle can accommodate it before pickup.

Do you have car seats for kids?

Car-seat availability and requirements still need to be confirmed. Include the number and ages of children in your request.

Do you pick up at the airport?

Yes. Airport transfers are available for travelers heading to and from St. Petersburg and Clearwater Beaches. Include the airport, flight timing, pickup location, and luggage details so coverage can be confirmed.

How do I pay?

Payment methods are still being finalized. Payment details will be included when your ride is confirmed.

What if my plans change?

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to adjust or cancel a confirmed ride. The final cancellation policy will be posted here.
